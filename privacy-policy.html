1. Introduction

Welcome to Tap Fast. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use our mobile application.

App Details:
Name: Tap Fast
Package ID: com.dhm.tapfast
Developer: Ahmed Amin / DHM

We are committed to protecting your privacy. Tap Fast is a casual reaction and tapping game designed for entertainment. By using the app, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

2. Data Collection & Usage

Tap Fast is designed to function without requiring user registration or accounts. We do not directly request or collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or government ID.

Local Storage

The app may store gameplay-related information locally on your device to ensure a consistent experience. This includes:

  • High scores and achievements
  • Game progress
  • Settings and preferences

Permissions

We do not request access to sensitive device features or data, including but not limited to:

  • Contacts, SMS, or Call Logs
  • Photos, Videos, Camera, or Microphone
  • Precise GPS location data
  • Payment or bank information

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Tap Fast uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads). This allows us to offer the game for free.

Google AdMob and related advertising partners may process technical information to serve ads, measure performance, and prevent fraud. This information may include:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location (city/region level)
  • Device information (model, OS version)
  • Interaction data (which ads were viewed or clicked)
  • Technical and diagnostic data

Personalized vs. Non-Personalized Ads

Depending on your region, consent choices, and device settings, you may receive "personalized" ads (based on your interests) or "non-personalized" ads. You can often manage these choices through your device settings (e.g., "Reset Advertising ID" or "Opt-out of Ads Personalization" on Android).

4. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used by Tap Fast is Google AdMob. We do not share your data with other third parties. For more information on how Google handles data, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google AdMob Information

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Since we do not maintain an online database of users or personal accounts for Tap Fast, we do not store your personal data on our servers.

Managing Local Data: You can remove gameplay data stored locally on your device at any time by:

  • Clearing the app's storage/cache in your device settings.
  • Uninstalling the application.

Information processed by Google AdMob is subject to Google's own retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

Tap Fast does not intentionally request or collect personal information from children through core gameplay. Our game is a general-audience casual game. However, because we use third-party advertising, the treatment of data for children is subject to Google AdMob policies and applicable laws (such as COPPA or GDPR-K).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us at the email provided below.
